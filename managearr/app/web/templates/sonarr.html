Import-failure reason policy

    
Sonarr-only. Observes completed/import-blocked/warning queue records and their Sonarr-reported rejection reason(s). Every reason defaults to "leave in queue"; automatic removal is a separate switch, disabled by default. "Unknown" and any message that cannot be matched to a known reason are never eligible for automatic removal, no matter what is selected below. Removal (when it fires) reuses the exact same bounded Sonarr queue DELETE as the slow-download guard above - there is no force-import and no other Sonarr write here.
